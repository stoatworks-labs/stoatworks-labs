<h1 align="center">Stoatworks Labs</h1>
<p align="center"><em>Open tools for the people who run the show.</em></p>

Stoatworks Labs is one working AV technician writing the software our industry never
got round to writing — and, increasingly, designing the hardware to go with it.
Audio, video, RF, content and show control. Free, open source, and shipped from the
same road cases you use.

**No account, no cloud, no phone-home.** These run on your machine, on your network,
on your show. A tool that stops working when the venue WiFi does is not a tool.

**Honest status, always.** Every repo says plainly what has been tested against real
hardware and what has only ever run in simulation. *Verified* and *assumed* are
different words and we use them differently.
